2:T517,Aspiring to become an actor I was desperately looking for a Theatre group to start my journey. After enquiring at several places and contacting multiple people, I came across a post of TheatreNama workshop and called them immediately. Since I wasn't well versed in Hindi I was in a dilemma whether to join but I was so desperate that I just wanted to start and that's one of the best decision of my life. The heart of this group Aashish Rana widely known as Bawa who's been like my brother supported me right from the start. Despite not being fluent in Hindi he trusted me and offered me to stage in a play like “Maharathi” within a week of joining and ever since then he's constantly been there and I've performed in nine productions so far playing roles like “Ramchandar”, “Rajguru”, “Secret Agent 303” “Anthony” and so on. What was a group of 25 people when I joined is now one of the fastest growing theatre groups in Bengaluru with more than 150 people. For me, Theatrenama is not just a theatre group but also my second family and Home. No matter what your profession or age is, no matter what your barriers are, Theatrenama welcomes everyone and provides them a chance to fulfill their passion and dreams. The only thing you need to pay them is your dedication and commitment.3:T437,Being a part of Theatrenama for over two years has been an enriching journey, akin to finding solace in a second home, especially after a rough day at the office. Venturing into professional Hindi theatre seemed like a distant dream, particularly in a city like Bangalore where Hindi isn't widely spoken. Yet, Theatrenama's steadfast presence and unwavering support have not only provided me with the opportunity to learn but also to earn a livelihood. The trust placed in individuals based not just on their skills, but also on their dedication and hard work, is commendable. Performing on prestigious stages like Rangashankara has been surreal, and having the founder's unwavering belief in me, regardless of the possibility of mistakes, is truly humbling. Theatrenama has become my sanctuary, where I can escape from life's challenges and immerse myself in the world of theatre.
