About Theatrenama

Our aim is to bring the magic of theatre to all.

Founded and based in Bengaluru since 2021, our aim is to bring the magic of theatre to all. Our diverse ensemble of talented and passionate individuals is dedicated to showcasing new talent and delivering a varied mix of genres from comedies to thrillers, murder mysteries, mythological, patriotic and more. Join us on our quest to bring the power of theatre to everyone!

Acting classes

Theatre-based acting training

Learn from Bangalore’s best and experienced theatre professionals through hands-on training, live stage practice, and personalized guidance.

  • Theatre-Based Acting Techniques — Master Navarasa expression, movement, and dialogue through live practice.
  • On-Camera Training — Learn screen presence and confidence for film & digital platforms.
  • Voice & Body Language Coaching — Build stage control and emotional expression.
  • Character Building & Improvisation — Explore your creativity and instincts as a performer.
  • Performance Opportunities — Act in real plays and short films produced by Theatrenama Directors.
