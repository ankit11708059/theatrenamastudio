Why Theatrenama

Why choose Theatrenama acting classes?

At Theatrenama, acting is not just taught, it’s lived.

With years of experience performing and directing in the Bangalore theatre scene, our mentors bring authenticity and depth to every session.

You’ll experience:

  • 01

    Theatre-Based Acting Techniques

    Master Navarasa expression, movement, and dialogue through live practice.

  • 02

    On-Camera Training

    Learn screen presence and confidence for film & digital platforms.

  • 03

    Voice & Body Language Coaching

    Build stage control and emotional expression.

  • 04

    Character Building & Improvisation

    Explore your creativity and instincts as a performer.

  • 05

    Performance Opportunities

    Act in real plays and short films produced by Theatrenama Directors.

The curriculum

What you'll learn?

“As Charlie once said, you'll remember it, if it was worth remembering.”

Our structured curriculum helps both beginners and aspiring professionals discover their true artistic side.

Modules include:

  1. Scene 1Basics of Acting & Stage Presence
  2. Scene 2Improvisation and Scene Work
  3. Scene 3Script Reading & Dialogue Delivery
  4. Scene 4Character Study & Acting methods by Chekov, Stanislavsky
  5. Scene 5Acting for Camera & Auditions
  6. Scene 6Theatre Production & Group Performances

Each session blends practical performance with personal feedback, ensuring holistic growth.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can join Theatrenama’s acting classes?+

Anyone above 16 with interest in acting, theatre, or performance can join — no prior experience required.

Do you provide on-camera acting training?+

Yes, our special sessions include screen acting and audition practice.

Where are the acting classes held?+

At Theatrenama Studio, Koramangala, Bangalore.

Do students get to perform live?+

Absolutely! While the student attends workshop, he/she can join any theatre productions parallelly.
